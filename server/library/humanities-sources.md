# Humanities and values Library: editorial sources

Checked 9 September 2026. `humanities.json` contains 35 original supplementary units: Islam and Moral at d1–d6/t1–t5, History at d4–d6/t1–t5, and Geography at t1–t5. Each unit has a note, separate reading and independent practice. These are selected learning topics, not a complete official syllabus or official examination questions.

All teaching paragraphs, scenarios, numbers, questions, distractors, hints and explanations were newly written. The existing `values.ts` and `scienceHumanities.ts` banks were consulted only for age/topic orientation. Source wording and existing bank questions were not reproduced. Historical documents and numerical case studies invented for exercises are explicitly labelled rekaan or simulasi. Their people, records and figures are not asserted to be archival discoveries or measured research findings.

## Moral education editorial boundaries

The eleven Moral units are original reasoning scenarios rather than external factual claims or statements of current law. They progress from caring for shared materials and listening, through repair, access and permission, to peer pressure, consultation, evaluation of environmental claims, conflicts of interest and ethical allocation. Photo consent is taught as respectful interpersonal practice, not a claim about a particular statute. Scenarios about school policies state the applicable invented rule explicitly. Environmental examples distinguish measured and unmeasured effects rather than presenting unsupported lifecycle rankings.

## Validation

All 210 prompts are unique and none matches a question prompt in the existing two subject-bank files. Correct positions occur 39, 55, 59 and 57 times for indices 0–3; no practice has all four answers in one position. Early-primary notes contain 56–68 words and readings 46–61; later notes contain 100–121 words and readings 63–85. Twenty-six numerical results were independently recomputed, and choices were reviewed against the self-contained contexts. No third-party passages, production records, paid AI calls or RDC material were used.
