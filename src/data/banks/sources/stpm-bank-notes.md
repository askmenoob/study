- [Darul Quran JAKIM's Nota Rujukan Tajwid](https://www.darulquran.gov.my/index.php/muatturun/nota-rujukan-sijil-tahfiz-malaysia/105-nota-rujukan-tajwid-stm) confirms the mad lazim kalimi muthaqqal classification and six-harakat reading used in the question involving الضَّالِّينَ. ## Content and ambiguity review

Every question has four distinct choices, one keyed answer and an explanation. Chinese, Tamil and Arabic items retain the relevant scripts in prompts, choices and explanations. Literary interpretation questions ask for the interpretation best supported by the supplied text, rather than presenting an unsupported universal meaning as fact.

Numerical and model-dependent questions state relevant conventions: midpoint elasticity; a closed economy without government for the multiplier; continuous output for derivatives; annual effective interest and compounding; independence for binomial probability; the small-angle wave approximation; work done by the gas as positive; non-relativistic particle motion; concentration-based chemistry and weak-acid approximations; Hardy–Weinberg conditions; and the Fick units. Accounting items specify the applicable practice policy, timing or absence of other adjustments.

The landing-force item explicitly asks about average **net force**, not ground reaction force including body weight. The RGB item explicitly concerns additive light, avoiding pigment conventions. The question about demographic dependency states age bands and distinguishes age from actual employment. These qualifications prevent common alternative answers.

## Limits

This is a selective pre-university practice supplement, not a complete official syllabus, an official paper, a semester-by-semester mapping, or a prediction of exam questions. The 184 added items are separate from the existing bank. No claim is made that eight MCQs per subject replace essays, practical work, coursework, prescribed-text study, oral performance or supervised tahfiz assessment.

Editorial self-review and the stated specialist source checks have been completed. Independent subject-teacher moderation, empirical difficulty calibration, and native-speaker external review have not been performed. Recitation questions support conceptual learning and do not certify the quality of a student's spoken recitation.
